In Simple Words / सरल भाषा में

This message is completely fake. The government is NOT giving ₹5,000 or free laptops. Anyone asking you to forward this link to WhatsApp groups is tricking you into visiting a dangerous scam website that steals your private data. Do not click the link, and do not forward it to family or friends.

Civic Safety Tip: Always verify before forwarding

Scammers frequently use emotional triggers like "Free money" or "Last day to apply" along with urgent requests to "Forward to 5 WhatsApp groups" to spread malware links. Legitimate government schemes are always announced on official .gov.in or .nic.in portals.
